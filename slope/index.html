HOW TO PLAY

Your ball rolls down an endless slope through a city of neon towers. Steer to stay on the rooftops and avoid anything red. Every obstacle you get through is a point. Each one is its own building with a drop before it, ramps launch you across the bigger gaps, and each speed tunnel makes you a lot faster. On tilted roofs, stay on the high side.

A D / ← →Steer
SPACE / W / ↑Jump (Plus mode)
SHIFT / S / ↓Dash — a quick burst that lets you phase through red for a moment. Hold a direction to side-step. Works in the air too.
ESC / PPause
RQuick restart
MMute

Plus mode adds gaps you must jump, hurdles, dash-gates you can only pass by phasing, and blue shields that save you from one hit.

Classic mode is the original: steering only, every section can be survived without jumping.

Secrets: the green roof over a red tunnel is rideable, and so is the outside of a speed tunnel (roll up its side and over the top). There are coins up there, and in Plus, power-ups: shield, coin magnet, 2x points and slow-mo.

Phones & tablets: hold the left or right half of the screen to steer. Swipe up to jump, swipe down to dash (or use the buttons). Gamepads work too.
